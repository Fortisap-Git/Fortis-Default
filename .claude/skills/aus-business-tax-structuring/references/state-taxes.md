## 2. Land tax: the SPV question

Land tax is assessed on the aggregated unimproved (site) value of all land an owner holds in that state at the assessment date (31 December in NSW and VIC, 30 June in QLD). The owner type sets the threshold and surcharge. This is the annual cost of holding property in an entity and it must be in every SPV comparison. Take the land value from the council rates notice or the Valuer-General notice on the file.

### NSW (2026 land tax year, thresholds frozen)

| Owner | Threshold | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Individual, company, fixed trust, unit trust (fixed) | General $1,075,000 | $100 plus 1.6% of value above threshold; above premium $6,571,000: $88,036 plus 2% |
| Special trust (discretionary, non-fixed unit trust) | None | 1.6% from the first dollar to $6,571,000, then $105,136 plus 2% |
| Foreign person (individual, company, trust with any foreign beneficiary not irrevocably excluded) | None | Surcharge 5% of residential land value, on top of ordinary land tax |

Related companies are grouped and share one threshold. A discretionary trust deed must irrevocably exclude foreign persons to avoid the 5% surcharge and the 9% surcharge purchaser duty. Principal place of residence exemption is not available to a company or discretionary trust. Boarding houses meeting the annual tariff ruling (LT119 for 2026) and low cost accommodation (LT120) can be exempt: at least 80% of occupied accommodation to long term residents at or below the published tariffs, with an annual declaration.

## 3. Transfer duty on moving an asset into an entity

Duty is payable on the greater of consideration and unencumbered market value on every transfer of land to a related company or trust, in every state. There is no general "family restructure" exemption. Valuation evidence under three months old is required for related party transfers in QLD and expected elsewhere.

### Corporate reconstruction and restructure relief

Every state has a corporate reconstruction exemption or concession for transfers within a corporate group (NSW 90% concession on the duty, VIC 90% concession, QLD full exemption with conditions). They need a group of companies, or companies and unit trusts, under 90% common ownership, before and usually for a period after. They do not cover an individual moving land into a company or trust, and they do not cover a discretionary trust restructure. Apply before the transfer; relief is not retrospective.

## 4. GST at the state boundary

Duty is calculated on the GST-inclusive consideration where the supply is taxable. If a rooming house is commercial residential premises, the transfer price for duty includes any GST unless the going concern exemption applies.

## 5. Checklist for any SPV or restructure

- [ ] State of each parcel confirmed from the title or rates notice, not the client's address.
- [ ] Land value per parcel from the latest rates or Valuer-General notice, aggregated with other land of the same owner in that state.
- [ ] Land tax for the current owner and each candidate owner, per year, in the model.
- [ ] Trust deed excludes foreign persons irrevocably (NSW, VIC) or the surcharge is costed.
- [ ] Duty on the transfer at market value, with valuation evidence; corporate reconstruction relief considered where a corporate group exists.
- [ ] Landholder duty if the plan is to move shares or units rather than land.
- [ ] Payroll tax grouping across the new group.
- [ ] Boarding house, rooming house, primary production or build-to-rent exemptions checked in the relevant state.
- [ ] Land tax registration lodged for the new owner in each state.
