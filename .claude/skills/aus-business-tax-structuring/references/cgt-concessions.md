# CGT small business concessions (Division 152) and the general discount

Preserved by the 2026-27 Budget. These apply to **active assets** of a business. A passive residential rental is never an active asset, so none of this applies to a client transferring an investment property. Business premises used by a connected entity's business do qualify.

## Basic conditions (all concessions)

1. A CGT event happens to a CGT asset and produces a gain.
2. One of: the entity is a CGT small business entity (aggregated turnover under $2m); the maximum net asset value test is met ($6m net assets of the entity, affiliates and connected entities, excluding the home, super and personal use assets); or the asset is used in a connected entity's or affiliate's small business.
3. Active asset test: active for at least half the ownership period, or 7.5 years if held over 15 years. Goodwill is always active. Land and buildings are active if used in a business (not if the main use is to derive rent, unless the rent is from a connected entity's business). Shares and units are active only where 80% of the underlying assets are active, and additional conditions apply (significant individual 20%, CGT concession stakeholder).

## The four concessions, in order

1. **15 year exemption** (Subdiv 152-B): asset held continuously 15 years; individual 55 or over and the event is in connection with retirement, or permanently incapacitated; for a company or trust, a significant individual throughout and a CGT concession stakeholder 55 or over retiring. Whole gain disregarded, and the proceeds can go to super under the CGT cap ($1.865m for 2025-26, verify 2026-27).
2. **50 per cent active asset reduction** (Subdiv 152-C): after the general discount. Can be skipped to preserve more for the retirement exemption.
3. **Retirement exemption** (Subdiv 152-D): lifetime cap $500,000 per individual. Under 55 the amount must go into super as a non-concessional contribution under the CGT cap; 55 or over may take it in cash.
4. **Rollover** (Subdiv 152-E): defer the remaining gain by acquiring a replacement active asset within one year before to two years after; if none is acquired the gain crystallises at the end of the period.

## Stacking

```
Capital gain
  less general 50% discount (individual or trust, held 12 months; to 30 June 2027, then indexation and 30% minimum)
  less 50% active asset reduction
  less retirement exemption (to $500k lifetime)
  less rollover
= net capital gain
```

Example: $1,200,000 gain, individual. Discount to $600,000, active asset reduction to $300,000, retirement exemption of $300,000 within the cap, nil taxable. From 1 July 2027 the first step changes; model both.

## Traps

- The $6m test counts connected entities and affiliates, including the spouse's assets where the spouse is an affiliate. Investment properties in the family group can push the group over.
- Rent to third parties makes land passive. Rent to a connected entity that uses the land in its business keeps it active.
- "In connection with retirement" for the 15 year exemption is looked at closely; reducing hours materially counts, a paper retirement does not.
- Retirement exemption under 55: the super contribution must be made by the later of 30 days after receiving the proceeds and the lodgement day.
- Concessions through a trust need the beneficiary to be an individual or, for the retirement exemption, a CGT concession stakeholder; check the trust deed permits capital distributions.
- Earnout rights: look-through treatment under Subdiv 118-I changes timing.
- The trust minimum tax from 2028 (announced) applies to trust net income; how it interacts with concessional capital gains distributed to individuals is in the exposure draft and should be checked when advising a trust sale after that date.

## Small business restructure rollover (Subdiv 328-G)

Available to small business entities (turnover under $10m) and their connected entities and affiliates for a genuine restructure with no change in ultimate economic ownership. Rolls over CGT, trading stock, depreciating assets and revenue assets. A discretionary trust can satisfy the ultimate economic ownership test through a family trust election. No duty relief. Not available for passive investment assets.

## The general 50 per cent discount (Div 115)

Individuals and trusts, asset held at least 12 months, resident. Not companies. Under the announced 2027 regime it is replaced for gains accruing from 1 July 2027 by indexation plus a 30 per cent minimum tax; gains accrued to 30 June 2027 keep it. See `law-changes-2026.md`.
