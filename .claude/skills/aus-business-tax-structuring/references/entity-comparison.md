# Entity comparison, 2026-27

## Tax rates

| Entity | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Individual (resident) | 0 / 15 / 30 / 37 / 45% plus 2% Medicare | 15% band falls to 14% from 1 July 2027 |
| Company, base rate entity | 25% | Aggregated turnover under $50m and passive income no more than 80% of assessable income. A pure rental company fails |
| Company, other | 30% | |
| Discretionary trust | Beneficiaries' rates; undistributed income at 47% | From 1 July 2028 (announced) a 30% minimum tax at trustee level with credits to individual beneficiaries only |
| Fixed unit trust | Unitholders' rates | Excluded from the trust minimum tax |
| Partnership | Partners' rates | Not a taxpayer |
| SMSF | 15% accumulation, 0% pension phase; Div 296 on balances above $3m from 1 July 2026 | |

## CGT

| Entity | Now | From 1 July 2027 (announced) |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | 50% discount after 12 months | CPI indexation of cost base plus 30% minimum tax on real gains; discount kept on gain accrued to 30 June 2027 |
| Company | No discount | No change; 25% or 30% on nominal gain |
| Trusts (all) | 50% discount flows to individual beneficiaries | As individual, through the trust |
| SMSF | One third discount | Unchanged |
| Small business concessions (Div 152) | Available to all on active assets | Preserved |

## Asset protection

| Entity | From personal creditors of the owner | For business creditors of the entity |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader | None | None |
| Partnership | None; joint and several | None |
| Company | Shares are exposed; assets inside are not | Limited liability, pierced by guarantees, insolvent trading, director penalty notices |
| Discretionary trust | Strong; beneficiaries own nothing | Trustee liable; corporate trustee limits it |
| Unit trust | Units are exposed | As discretionary trust |
| SMSF | Very strong (Bankruptcy Act) | n/a |

## Income splitting and retention

| Entity | Splitting | Retaining at low rates |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader | No | No |
| Partnership | Fixed by agreement | No |
| Company | Salary (deductible), dividends by share class | Yes, 25% or 30% |
| Discretionary trust | Flexible now; from 2028 minimum 30% at trustee level, credits to individuals | Via corporate beneficiary now; not effective after 2028 (no credit) |
| Unit trust | Fixed by units | No |
| SMSF | n/a | Yes, 15% |

## Setup and running costs (indicative, confirm against Fortis pricing)

| Entity | Setup | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader | ABN only | $500 to $1,500 |
| Partnership | $1,000 to $2,000 | $1,500 to $3,000 |
| Company | $1,500 to $2,500 (ASIC registration fee included) | $2,500 to $5,000 plus ASIC annual review about $320 |
| Discretionary or unit trust with corporate trustee | $2,500 to $4,500 | $3,000 to $6,000 |
| Trust plus operating company | $5,000 to $8,000 | $6,000 to $12,000 |
| SMSF | $3,000 to $5,000 | $3,000 to $6,000 plus audit |

## Structures for a trading business, after the Budget

**A. Sole trader.** Under about $80k profit, low liability, no assets to protect. Review yearly.

**B. Operating company owned by the individuals.** The new default for most owner-operated businesses: 25% base rate entity, salary and franked dividends, Div 7A discipline, straightforward for lenders and investors. Add a holding company when retained earnings or IP need separating from trading risk (inter-company dividends are frankable and rebatable).

**C. Operating company owned by a discretionary trust.** Still works to 30 June 2028 for distributing franked dividends to family members. After that the minimum tax applies at trustee level with credits only to individuals, so the benefit narrows to families whose beneficiaries would pay 30% or more anyway. Consider the EET election or rolling the trust's shares into individual or holding company ownership during the 2027 to 2030 window.

**D. Fixed unit trust with corporate trustee.** For unrelated co-owners and for property held with others. Excluded from the trust minimum tax.

**E. Discretionary trust trading directly.** Only where asset protection and succession are the reasons and the family accepts the 30% minimum from 2028. Plan for the rollover window if a company would serve better.

**F. SMSF holding business real property** leased to the operating entity at market rent. Strong for owners over 45 with a long horizon; watch Div 296 above $3m.

## Decision tree, 2026-27

```
Is there liability risk, or assets worth protecting, or profit above ~$80k?
  No  -> sole trader, review annually
  Yes -> Are there or will there be unrelated co-owners or investors?
           Yes -> company (share classes) or fixed unit trust
           No  -> Is retaining profit at 25% the main aim?
                    Yes -> operating company owned by individuals (add HoldCo if assets or IP to separate)
                    No  -> Is income splitting to lower-rate family members worth more than the 2028 minimum tax costs?
                             Yes -> discretionary trust (EET election or restructure rollover kept in view)
                             No  -> operating company
Property alongside the business -> own name or unit trust for residential; SMSF for business premises; never the trading entity
```
