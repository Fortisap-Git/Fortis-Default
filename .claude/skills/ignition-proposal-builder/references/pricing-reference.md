# Fortis pricing reference for Ignition proposals

Built 2026-09-10 from the Ignition service library (58 services) and the FY26 season of accepted proposals (100 accepted between 2 July and 9 September 2026, PROP-1648 to PROP-2014, with 13 read in full). All figures are ex GST. ## 1. Why the check exists

Two pricing behaviours sit side by side in the accepted FY26 proposals:

- **New clients are priced from the components.** Nami (PROP-1990) and Nicholas (PROP-2001) are the same job shape and both landed at exactly $1,950. Hearn & Bines (PROP-1725) at $2,500 sits within 3% of the component sum. Martin (PROP-1984), Caraher SMSF (PROP-2014) and 2R Design (PROP-1786) all sit within 10% of it.
- **Existing clients are anchored to last year's fee plus an uplift.** Ayres (PROP-1988) prices are visibly prior-year figures times 1.06 (934 to 990, 566 to 600, 255 to 270). Amcorp (PROP-1922) bundles a company, two individuals and three rental schedules into one $3,500 line, about 68% of the component sum. GRADE-A (PROP-1986) covers a trading company, a trading trust with corporate trustee and two individuals for $3,500, about 45% of the component sum.

The uplift-only path keeps a legacy fee legacy forever. The check below puts a component build-up and two or three real comparables next to the uplifted figure so Rehman can see the gap at the confirm step and decide. It recommends; it never overrides.

## 4. The mechanism: build, compare, recommend

Run this at step 2 of every build, after the Monday fee (or Rehman's stated fee) is known and before the confirmation.

1. **Build the reference fee.** From the entity list you resolved in step 1, sum the Reference column for each entity and each schedule you know about (rentals, sole trader, CGT, R&D, payroll). Where you do not know a detail that changes the number (is the company trading or dormant, is the sole trader GST registered), assume the standard row and say so.
2. **Compute the proposed fee.** Existing client: Monday last FY fee times 1.05, rounded per section 5. New client: the reference fee, rounded, unless Rehman has given a figure.
3. **Compare.** Ratio = proposed fee divided by reference fee.
   - **0.85 to 1.20: consistent.** Say so in one line and move on.
   - **Below 0.85: recommend higher.** State the reference fee, the gap in dollars, and a recommended fee. For a new client recommend the reference fee rounded. For an existing client recommend the reference fee rounded, and also show the halfway step (midpoint of proposed and reference, rounded) as the softer option, since a jump of 40% or more on a long-standing client is a relationship call Rehman should make with both numbers in front of him.
   - **Above 1.20: check for drivers before recommending lower.** Look through the entity scope for the drivers in section 6. If they account for the gap, say "above reference, explained by [drivers]" and treat as consistent. If they do not, recommend the reference fee rounded, or ask what is driving the premium.
4. **Quote comparables.** Two or three rows from section 3 with the closest mix, by number and fee. If nothing in the table resembles the group, say so rather than forcing a comparison, and offer to read one of the named proposals in section 3 with `get_proposal_document`.
5. **Present, do not decide.** The confirmation shows the proposed fee, the reference fee, the comparables and the recommendation on one screen. Rehman picks the number that goes into the draft. Never build on the recommended figure without his say-so, and never silently substitute it for the Monday figure.

## 5. Rounding rule

Every fee that goes into a proposal is rounded to the nearest $50 ex GST. Midpoints round up. Round the total, not the components; the GST resolves on the rounded ex GST figure via the tax slug.

Examples: 20,000 x 1.05 = 21,000 (already round). 2,344 x 1.05 = 2,461.20 rounds to 2,450. 7,600 x 1.05 = 7,980 rounds to 8,000. 2,375 rounds to 2,400. 1,225 rounds to 1,250.

Python one-liner if you want it: `int((x + 25) // 50 * 50)`.

## 6. Complexity drivers that legitimately lift a fee

Presence of these in the scope explains a fee above the component build-up. Note which ones apply when you report an above-reference ratio.

- Payroll and superannuation review each quarter, STP finalisation
- R&D tax incentive schedule
- CGT event on sale of a business or property (as distinct from a share parcel)
- Three or more rental properties in the group
- Bookkeeping or record reconstruction folded into the fixed fee (normally quoted separately; flag if it is inside)
- Part-year entities, cessations, registrations to cancel
- First-year entities needing opening balances established
- Multiple bank, loan and intercompany accounts across the group
- Large or multi-state trading company, payroll tax
- Multiple years being brought up to date inside one engagement

## 7. Worked examples

**Existing group, board fee 7,600, company plus investment trust plus two individuals.**
Reference: 3,500 + 2,000 + 760 = 6,260. Proposed: 7,600 x 1.05 = 7,980, rounds to 8,000. Ratio 1.28. Drivers found: company has payroll review and STP in scope. Report: "8,000 proposed. Reference build-up 6,260. Above reference, explained by payroll and STP. Comparables PROP-1923 Greensmith 8,200 (larger mix, R&D), PROP-1986 GRADE-A 3,500 (same mix, legacy anchor). Consistent."

**Existing group, board fee 3,300, company plus trading trust plus two individuals (the GRADE-A shape).**
Reference: 3,500 + 3,500 + 760 = 7,760. Proposed: 3,300 x 1.05 = 3,465, rounds to 3,450. Ratio 0.44. Report: "3,450 proposed. Reference build-up 7,760. Well below reference. Recommend 7,750, or 5,600 as a halfway step this year. Comparable PROP-1986 GRADE-A accepted 3,500 on this mix, so the legacy anchor is real; your call on pace."

**New client, two individuals with trust distributions and a first-year investment trust with corporate trustee.**
Reference: 900 + 1,200 + 0 = 2,100, rounds to 2,100. Comparables PROP-1990 and PROP-2001 both accepted at 1,950 on the identical mix. Report: "Reference 2,100. Two identical FY26 jobs accepted at 1,950. Recommend 1,950 for consistency, or 2,100 if you want to move the standard up."

## 8. Maintaining this file

When a build closes with an agreed fee that is not in section 3, add a row: reference, client, mix, fee, ratio, one-word read. When the library prices change (Rehman updates a service in Ignition), update the Library column and reconsider the Reference column. If three or more new comparables in a row land outside a Reference value by more than 20% for the same reason, change the Reference value and note the date and reason here. This file is the memory that keeps quotes consistent; a fee looked up once should inform the next one.
